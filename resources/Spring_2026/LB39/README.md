**Speaker**: Marybeth Arcodia (ATM/IDSC)

**Title**: PyTorch 101: A Practical Introduction for Earth Scientists

**Abstract**: This talk introduces PyTorch, a flexible deep learning framework that's gaining traction for its flexibility and utility in building machine learning prediction models. We'll cover what PyTorch is, why it matters for Earth science applications, and how it compares to its competitors like TensorFlow and JAX. The presentation starts with fundamentals, then progresses to more technical implementation details. We'll explore practical examples relevant to Earth science problems, with short coding demonstrations throughout. Bring your laptop to follow along with hands-on examples and bring your lunch to eat. No prior machine learning experience required.
