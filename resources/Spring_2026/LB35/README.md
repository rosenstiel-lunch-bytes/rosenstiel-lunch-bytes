**Speaker**: Milan Curcic (OCE/IDSC)

**Title**: *What Every Scientist Should Know About AI in 2026*

**Abstract**: Large Language Models (LLMs) are rapidly growing in size and capability and are transforming modern knowledge work. AI agents, which are essentially multi-step pipelines of LLM inference, have been demonstrated to successfully carry out complex knowledge work tasks in minutes, and which would otherwise require hours from a human. Due to the rapid pace of AI development and the overwhelm of information and tools out there, it may be difficult to understand how to accelerate your work with AI in an effective, responsible, and safe way. In this Lunch Byte seminar, I will in simple words explain what is AI, LLMs, AI Agents, Pretraining, Inference, and Context windows, and more. I will also show you how to get started with this transformative technology while remaining in the driver's seat.
