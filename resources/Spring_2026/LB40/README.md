**Speaker**: Michael Fischer (ATM/IDSC)

**Title**: The vibes are immaculate, but is the research? A reflection on vibe coding for scientists.

**Abstract**: The emergence of AI-assisted coding tools has fundamentally changed what is possible for individual researchers who use programming languages to examine data. This talk presents a case study of TC-ATLAS, a web-based tropical cyclone research platform that synthesizes multi-platform observational datasets. Rather than a conventional tutorial, this talk explores the emerging paradigm of "vibe coding", typically defined as using AI agents to write, debug, and deploy software from natural language, through the lens of real development sessions. I will provide my thoughts on the strengths and weaknesses of such AI tools, including how they influence productivity and scientific reliability. My hope is to at least demonstrate what these AI tools are capable of and, possibly, convince you that AI tools are legitimate accelerators in the research process.
