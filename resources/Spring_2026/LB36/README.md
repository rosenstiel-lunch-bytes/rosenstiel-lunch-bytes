**Speaker**: Brian McNoldy (ATM)

**Title**: *Plots & Pixels: Three Decades of Building and Sharing Science Products on the Web*

**Abstract**: Science communication has been a passion of mine since I was in high school in the early 1990s. Although the technology landscape has evolved significantly since then, my goal has not: strive to make science content that is approachable and understandable. In this presentation, I will take you on a visual tour of my website history, which began 32 years ago when websites were few and in their infancy. I will share my design principles and tools, and highlight real-time products that I offer on my website. I will also present some options for anyone who might be interested in creating a website with dynamic content.
